---
title: Handling replies in the Unibox
summary: One inbox for every reply, sorted by AI.
order: 7
---
Outreach → Unibox collects replies to your campaigns from every mailbox, checked every 5 minutes. Your other mail is never stored.

- A real reply stops that lead's sequence (if "stop on reply" is on) and marks the lead Replied.
- Out-of-office replies are detected and don't stop anything.
- With AI turned on, replies are labeled (interested, meeting, not now, not interested...) and a draft answer can be written for you.
- Reply from the Unibox: it goes out from the original mailbox, in the same thread.
