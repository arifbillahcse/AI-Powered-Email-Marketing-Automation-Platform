## Warmup

Turn warmup on per mailbox (Infrastructure → Warmup). Warmup mailboxes email each other short conversations; ours opens them, rescues them from spam, files them in a "Warmup" folder and replies to some. Volume starts at 2 a day and grows daily up to the target you set.

- Warm up new mailboxes for **2 to 4 weeks** before cold email.
- Keep warmup on while campaigns run.
- Watch the **inbox rate**: above 90% is healthy.

## Inbox placement tests

Infrastructure → Inbox placement → **New test** sends your real subject and body to seed inboxes at different providers, then shows inbox, spam or missing for each. Run one before a big launch, and after DNS changes.
