## Keep it short and personal

Three to five sentences, one clear question, no images or attachments in the first email. Plain text often does best.

## Variables and fallbacks

`{{first_name|there}}` uses the lead's first name, or "there" if it's empty. Every lead field works: `{{company}}`, `{{title}}`, `{{website}}`, and your custom fields.

## Spintax

`{Hi|Hello|Hey}` picks one option per lead, so emails don't look identical.

## Follow-ups

Leave a follow-up's subject empty to reply in the same thread. Most replies come from the second or third email.

## A/B tests

Add versions B, C... to any email. Each lead gets one version; the winner (by replies, opens or clicks) is picked automatically.

## LinkedIn and call steps

Change a step's type to a LinkedIn action, a call or a task. When a lead reaches it, a task appears in Outreach → Tasks, and the sequence waits until it's done.
