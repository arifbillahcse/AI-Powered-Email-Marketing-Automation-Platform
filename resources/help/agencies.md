## Client workspaces

Settings → Clients → **Add client** creates a separate workspace per client, on your plan. Invite the client with the read-only Client role if they should see results.

## Your brand

Workspace settings → Branding: your name, logo and color replace ours in the app, on reports and in invitation emails.

## Custom domain

Point a CNAME (e.g. `app.youragency.com`) to the platform, then click **Verify domain**. Your sign-in page there shows your brand.

## Client reports

Each client has a secret report link (no login needed) and optional weekly or monthly report emails.
