---
title: Getting started
summary: From a new account to your first campaign, step by step.
order: 1
---
The **Get started** checklist on your dashboard tracks these steps for you.

1. **Connect a mailbox.** Infrastructure → Email accounts. Use "Sign in with Google" or "Sign in with Microsoft", or add any inbox with its SMTP and IMAP settings.
2. **Check your DNS.** Infrastructure → Sending domains shows SPF, DKIM and DMARC for each domain you send from. Fix anything red before sending (see [DNS setup](/help/dns-setup)).
3. **Add your mailing address.** Workspace settings. Anti-spam law requires a postal address in every campaign email; we add it to the footer for you.
4. **Warm up new mailboxes.** Infrastructure → Warmup. Give a new mailbox two to four weeks before cold email.
5. **Import leads.** Outreach → Leads → Import (CSV or Excel), or use the Lead finder.
6. **Write a campaign.** Outreach → Campaigns → Template library is the fastest start. Replace the [bracketed] parts.
7. **Launch.** Pick the audience (lists or segments) and the mailboxes, preview a few leads, then launch.

Sending starts slowly and spreads across your mailboxes and the send window you chose.
