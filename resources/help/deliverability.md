---
title: Staying out of spam
summary: The rules that protect your sender reputation.
order: 6
---
- **Verify your list** before sending (Leads → Verify emails). Keep bounces under 3%.
- **Go slow:** 30 to 50 cold emails per mailbox per day. Add mailboxes to send more, not volume per mailbox.
- **Never buy lists.** Email people with a real reason to hear from you.
- **Honor unsubscribes** (automatic) and remove people who ask you to stop.
- **Avoid spammy words and links** in the first email; the spam checker in the editor flags them.
- **Use a custom tracking domain**, or turn open tracking off: shared tracking links can hurt.

We pause a mailbox or campaign automatically when bounces go over 5% or spam complaints over 0.3% in the last 7 days. Fix the cause (usually the list), then resume.
